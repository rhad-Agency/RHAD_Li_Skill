# RHAD (agency) brand guide for LinkedIn

Account: **RHAD, @rhad.agency, https://www.rhad.agency/**. Use this file for every post that is not about the RHAD45 product line.

Sources: RHAD EDGE type spec (`type-spec-rhad.md`), the 2026 RHAD social series, the RHAD website as audited 23 and 26 October 2026 (`rhad-master-art-direction-source.md`), and the standing rules the team uses on the RHAD account. RHAD has no single official brand manual. Where this file says "observed", it comes from the live site or shipped work, not a signed-off manual.

## Who RHAD is on LinkedIn

A commercially focused creative and growth agency working across Singapore, Indonesia, India, the UK and Australia. Confident, direct, practical. The founder's first-person voice is the strongest asset. Proof comes from real work for real clients, never from adjectives.

Clients that may be shown or referenced: **YHM, Swaraj, Beato**. Any other client needs the user's explicit OK. Never mock a client. Never invent a client result.

## Colour

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#1F2124` | Main text on white and light grey, dark surfaces |
| Ink soft | `#5B6169` | Notes, meta lines, captions |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Foundation. All text on red and on dark |
| RHAD red | `#D90A2C` | Accent: key words, rules, wordmark on dark. Sparingly |
| Red pressed | `#B00822` | Hover, pressed, small red text on white |
| On-dark soft | `#D6D7D9` | Body copy on dark |

Rules:

- White foundation, ink type, one red accent per slide or frame. Red is a signal, not a wash.
- Brand red `#D90A2C` on white is about 4.9:1. Fine for large text and key words. Use `#B00822` for small red text.
- No gradients, glows, glass, drop shadows or beige. Flat colour only.
- **Case-study posts** about another brand may borrow that brand's palette for the artwork, as in the "One brand, five borders" series (`assets/examples/carousel-example-five-borders.jpg`, which predates the no-numbers and no-category-labels rules: copy its look, not its page counters, year badges or country labels). Keep RHAD type, the RHAD footer line and the handle. Say so in the brief so it is a choice, not drift.

## Typography

Two families only, both OFL, files in `assets/fonts/`.

| Role | Family | Weights |
|---|---|---|
| Display: headlines, numbers, card titles | **Unbounded** | 600, 700, 800 |
| Text: body, labels, buttons, captions | **Plus Jakarta Sans** | 400, 500, 600, 700 |

Rules:

- Unbounded is for headlines, numbers and short titles. Never for paragraphs.
- Display type is tightened: -0.02em on small heads, -0.035em on section heads, up to -0.055em on a giant word. Body tracking is 0.
- Sentence case. All caps only for a single short label or one monument word.
- No italics anywhere.
- Display line height 0.85 to 1.2. Body 1.5 to 1.6.
- Full size table: `type-spec-rhad.md`. For social frames, scale from the 1080-wide canvas in `design-system.md`.

## Logo

Files in `assets/rhad/logos/`:

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `rhad-logo-red.png` | Default on white |
| `rhad-logo-white.png` | On ink or red |
| `rhad-logo-red-tight.png`, `rhad-logo-white-tight.png` | Same logos cropped to the artwork (the masters are 1080 square with heavy padding). Use these in layouts so size is predictable |
| `rhad-logo-thick.png` / `.svg` | Small sizes and avatar contexts where the fine strokes would break |

The logo is a red wordmark with an outline house symbol. The red inside the master PNG is its own artwork colour and slightly differs from the web CTA `#D90A2C`; do not recolour the file to match. Clear space equals the height of the "R". Never stretch, rotate, outline, shadow, recolour, or place on a busy photo. The logo is a sign-off, not a decoration: once per frame at most, usually bottom-left or in the footer.

## Voice

See `voice-and-tone.md`. Short version: short sentences, first person, one idea, plain words, no hype, no em dashes.

## Recurring devices

- **#100DaysOfRHAD** for series posts only. Do not add it to one-offs.
- **Footer line** on case-study carousels: `@rhad.agency` plus a one-line disclosure when another brand is shown ("Independent commentary. Not affiliated with X.") and an "as of" date for facts that change.
- **Mocha** (the dog) appears only in memes and reels, and only with the user. Never on a standard LinkedIn post, carousel or document.

## Do not

- Mix RHAD45 assets, the Join symbol or `#F03131` into an RHAD post
- Use pills, rounded tags or boxes behind text
- Use stock robots, AI orbs, fake dashboards, fabricated screenshots
- Quote a testimonial that is not in the brief
