# Voice and tone

Apply to every line: hook, body, slide text, captions, first comment, alt text, video on-screen text.

## RHAD (agency)

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Sentences | Short. Plain words. One idea per sentence |
| Person | First person "I" for founder posts. "We" for company-page posts. Pick one per post |
| One joke at most | Humour is dry and specific. Never at a client's, competitor's or a person's expense |
| Confidence | Say what happened and what it means. No hedging pile-ups, no hype |
| Proof | A real client, a real number, a real date. Or no claim |
| Close | One next step. Often a question a person could actually answer in a comment |
| Spelling | British / Singapore English: enquiry, colour, centre, organise |
| Format | Sentence case. No italics. No emoji spam (0 to 2, only if it adds meaning) |

**Never use:** em dashes. "Unlock", "elevate", "game-changer", "revolutionary", "leverage", "supercharge", "delve", "landscape", "in today's fast-paced world", "let me be honest", "here's the thing", "thread" emoji lead-ins, "the result?" reveals, "plot twist", engagement bait ("comment X to get Y", "agree?"), tag-farming.

Prefer: a plain verb, a number, a name, a place.

## RHAD45

Calm, precise, concrete. See `rhad45-brand.md` for approved lines and the banned list. No personality character. No comic or superhero language.

## Market safety

Posts reach SG, ID, IN, UK and AU. Before finalising check: idioms, religion and food references, politics, currency (S$, Rp, INR, £, A$), spelling, humour that lands in one market and misfires in another. If a line needs a market note, add it to `copy.md`.

## Hard stops

Politics, religion, tragedy, health scares, named private individuals, sexual or violent content, anything that punches at a client or a competitor by name, any unverified statistic, anyone's face or voice used without consent.

## Honest voice test

Before showing copy, read it as a sceptical SME owner in Singapore. Would they believe a person wrote it? Would they know what to do next? Delete every sentence that fails either.

## LinkedIn-specific tells to remove

Staccato fragment runs ("Short. Punchy. Done."), stacked triads, "Not X. Y." used more than once, performed vulnerability, a question as the first line, teaser phrases ("what nobody tells you"), a bridge before every reveal. One contrast and one triple per post at most. These are checked by `linkedin-humanizer` and `scripts/brand-lint.mjs`.
