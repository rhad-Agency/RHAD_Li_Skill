# RHAD45 brand guide for LinkedIn

RHAD45 is a **named product line within RHAD**: business automation products for Singapore service SMEs. Use this file only for posts about RHAD45. Tagline: "Business automation from RHAD". Working brand line: **Business, joined up.**

The full source documents are kept verbatim beside this file: `rhad45-brand-guide-source.md`, `rhad45-design-system-source.md`, `rhad45-brand-foundation-source.md`, `rhad45-brand-context-source.md`. Read them when this summary is not enough. Where they disagree with this file, the source wins, then tell the user.

## Offer facts (confirmed by the owner, 23 October 2026)

- Four products: **WhatsApp Lead Response, Loyalty Loop, Review Engine, Reporting & Recommendations** (Dashboard Reporting & Recommendations in the context file). Present all four as equals. Do not lead with WhatsApp.
- Price: **any one product S$100/month**. Setup, WhatsApp/API usage and third-party software are charged separately. Ongoing monitoring and support are included.
- Do not state a bundle price, a discount, setup amounts, tax treatment, contract or cancellation terms, support hours or response times. They are undefined.
- Two-month goal (~10 subscribers, S$1,000 MRR) is internal. Never present it as traction.
- CTA: **Arrange a 45-minute business review.** Submitting requests a review. It does not confirm an appointment.
- Parent agency work is agency experience, not RHAD45 outcomes. Label it so.

## Colour

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Brand red | `#F03131` | Logo, large display emphasis, rules |
| Action red | `#B62424` | Buttons, small red text, links |
| Action hover | `#951D1D` | Pressed states |
| Ink | `#191919` | Primary copy, dark surfaces |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Main surface, reverse copy |
| Paper | `#F5F3EF` | Alternating section background |
| Muted text | `#5B5B5B` | Secondary copy |
| Soft border | `#D8D3CC` | Dividers |

Brand red on white is about 4.05:1: logo and large text only. Normal-size red text uses action red (6.43:1). Colour never carries meaning alone. One red accent per frame. The 2026-10-26 direction removed the earlier gradient and miniature-diorama looks. White and neutral grey foundations, rectangular CTAs, sharp edges. Do not bring those back.

## Typography

Same two families as RHAD: **Unbounded 600-700** display, **Plus Jakarta Sans 400-700** body. Sentence case. Uppercase only for a short eyebrow label with generous tracking. No body in Unbounded. Headline tracking -0.025em.

## Logo

Files in `assets/rhad45/logos/` and `assets/rhad45/icons/`. Mark: **The Join**, the owner-edited symmetrical symbol.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `RHAD45-logo-primary` | Default on white or paper |
| `RHAD45-logo-reverse` | Red symbol and white wordmark on ink. Never use primary on ink |
| `RHAD45-logo-mono-ink / -white / -red` | Single-colour contexts. Mono-red is restricted |
| `RHAD45-icon-red / -white / -ink` | Avatars, small accents. Needs RHAD45 text nearby |

Clear space = half the symbol height. Full lockup min 180px wide on screen. Icon min 32px. Never stretch, skew, rotate, outline, shadow, redraw, replace the numerals, close the seam, or separate/animate the two forms. Never use the parent house icon in place of The Join. The lockup is a hybrid file (vector icon and numerals, raster parent lettering): do not describe it as fully vector or use it for large print.

## Imagery and graphic language

- Real people doing real business work, monochrome, with the restrained horizontal scanline displacement seen on the parent site. Never over copy or data.
- Flat red architectural module icons: 96-unit grid, 6-unit line, square terminals, angular geometry.
- Process and conversation visuals are built in HTML, not as fake screenshots. No fabricated dashboards, no invented customers or testimonials, no messaging-platform logos that imply endorsement, no stock robots or AI orbs.
- The Join may appear once as a small sign-off. Never tile it.

## Voice

Calm, precise, concrete. RHAD's commercial confidence expressed through everyday operational language. Short headlines, 2 to 3 sentence paragraphs. British/Singapore English (enquiries, centre, colour). Write **RHAD45** and **S$100/month** exactly. Numerals for prices, durations, steps. One problem, one product, one next step per post.

Approved lines: "Great businesses shouldn't run on 'I'll remember.'" / "Fewer loose ends. Stronger follow-through." / "Your enquiry flow captures the details. Your team takes the next step." / "One selected product. S$100/month. Ongoing monitoring and support."

### Banned for RHAD45

revolutionary, seamless with everything, guaranteed revenue, never miss a lead, unlimited, fully autonomous, instant bookings, five-star reviews guaranteed, no hidden fees, free setup, cancel anytime, 24/7 support (unless the user substantiates it), and any outcome guarantee (a booking, a sale, a rating, repeat revenue). State product boundaries next to the claim they limit:

| Product | Boundary to keep in view |
|---|---|
| WhatsApp Lead Response | Handoff does not guarantee a booking or sale |
| Loyalty Loop | Repeat revenue is measured, not guaranteed |
| Review Engine | No guaranteed rating or positive review |
| Reporting & Recommendations | Name the supported sources and scope |

Do not invent a meaning for "45".
