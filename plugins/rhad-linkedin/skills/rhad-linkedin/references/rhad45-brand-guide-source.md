SVG is preferred for the LP. The icon and numerals are vector; the parent lettering remains an embedded raster master. The SVG therefore is a hybrid file. PNG versions are supplied at twice the nominal dimensions for compatibility.

### Proportions, padding and minimum size

The lockup uses a 1100 × 280 viewBox. Preserve that ratio. Its visible symbol is 180 units high. Let X = half that symbol height (90 units). Keep at least X of clear space outside the visible artwork; the SVG canvas supplies part of that padding. Add container padding where needed. Do not crop a terminal or close the seam.

Digital baseline: full lockup at least 180 CSS px wide; typical header width 200–240px. Icon-only baseline: 32px square. At constrained mobile widths use a 180px lockup and a compact menu, rather than compressing the letters. The 16px browser favicon is a special size requiring browser inspection; it is not a general icon minimum. These are LP production rules, not print specifications.

Use the full name at first introduction. Icon-only use needs an adjacent account name or previously established brand context. Use image alt="RHAD45" when the logo identifies the brand. When the same link includes visible RHAD45 text, make the image decorative with alt="".

### Incorrect use

Do not stretch, skew, rotate, redraw, outline, add shadows, replace the numerals or alter the symbol's seam. Do not use the parent house icon in place of The Join for RHAD45. Do not place the lockup over a busy photo. Do not use the red primary lettering on an ink background: select the reverse asset. Do not animate the icon so its two forms separate or imply a loading/error state.

Brand red/white contrast is approximately 4.05:1. Reserve that combination for logos and large text; do not use it for ordinary body copy or normal-size button text. Decorative borders do not substitute for clear interactive boundaries.

This is an sRGB digital system. CMYK and Pantone equivalents are not specified; they require print profiling and proofing. Do not treat automatic conversions as approved spot colours.

Limit body text to about 60–68 characters per line. Headline tracking can be -0.025em; body tracking remains normal. Use sentence case; reserve uppercase for short eyebrow labels with generous tracking. Do not set body paragraphs in Unbounded or force hard line breaks that overflow mobile. Fallbacks: Arial/sans-serif for display, system-ui/sans-serif for body.

## Composition and imagery

Build confident, spacious layouts with strong left alignment and a limited number of red accents. White carries most reading; paper introduces section rhythm; ink anchors a demonstration or final CTA. Put the actual task and next action in the foreground.

Photography inherits the parent's observed monochrome character: real people doing relevant business work, useful context and honest scale. Horizontal distortion may be a restrained image treatment, never a readability effect on copy or data. The LP can ship without photography: use the supplied symbol and a real HTML workflow demonstration. Do not invent an AI dashboard screenshot or customer testimonial.

Product utility icons use a common 24 × 24 grid, approximately 2px strokes, consistent joins and simple silhouettes. Select one established icon library if needed. Keep logos separate from that utility icon set. Label the four products; do not use four unrelated colours or four new sub-brand logos.

## Motion

Use 120–200ms colour/border transitions. Optional section entrance: opacity plus at most 12px vertical travel over 240–320ms, once. All content must be visible without JS. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Avoid continuous logo rotation, bouncing icons, parallax and looping conversation animation. The first viewport should explain the offer immediately.

## Writing rules

Use British/Singapore English: enquiries, centre, colour. Write RHAD45 and S$100/month consistently. Use contractions and active verbs. Keep headlines short and body paragraphs to two or three sentences. Use numerals for prices, durations and workflow steps. Exclamation marks are rare. Explain product boundaries next to relevant claims.

Use: follow-through, enquiry, relevant follow-up, your team, agreed scope, clear next step, customer feedback, monitoring, support, practical automation.

Avoid: revolutionary, seamless with everything, guaranteed revenue, never miss a lead, unlimited, fully autonomous, instant bookings, five-star reviews guaranteed, no hidden fees, free setup, cancel anytime, 24/7 support unless specifically substantiated.

Support copy becomes calm and precise. Errors explain what happened and what the user can do. Submission success says the request was received only after the backend confirms receipt. It never promises a confirmed time.

## Brand in use

- Website: full primary lockup in header; reverse in dark footer; action red for buttons. Keep price exclusions visible.
- Social: icon with RHAD45 account name; one problem, one product, one next step per post.
- Email: compact full lockup, 16px body, clear sender and next action. No oversized signature artwork.
- Sales deck: one idea per slide, 40–56px display, minimum 20px body for projected use; parent proof labelled as agency work.
- Documents/print: white background and mono-ink alternative; obtain print masters and proof colours for physical production.
