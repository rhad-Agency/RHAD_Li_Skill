## 4. Brand architecture proposal

Treat **RHAD45 as a named product line within RHAD**. The parent already appears in the name; an additional 'by RHAD' lockup is optional and may be redundant. A provenance line can say 'Business automation from RHAD.'

```text
RHAD — parent agency
  RHAD45 — business automation product line
    WhatsApp Lead Response
    Loyalty Loop
    Review Engine
    Reporting & Recommendations
```

RHAD45 leads product communications. Descriptive product names explain the task each performs. RHAD marketing services remain separately scoped. Parent portfolio credentials must be labelled as RHAD agency experience.

Use RHAD45 consistently in running copy. The spacing and treatment of 45 can be explored visually. Its meaning is unconfirmed; do not invent a brand story about it.

## 5. Strategic foundation — proposals

**Problem:** Customer follow-through competes with the work of serving customers. The owner becomes the reminder system.

**Purpose:** Make it easier for a small business to keep everyday customer commitments.

**Vision:** A business can stay attentive without relying on its owner to remember every next step.

**Mission:** Turn recurring customer tasks into clear, supported routines that help small teams keep business moving.

| Value | Behaviour that would make it credible |
| --- | --- |
| Follow-through | Define the trigger, next step, responsible person and exception path |
| Clarity | Explain scope, costs and customer responsibilities before commitment |
| Accountability | Define monitoring and support ownership; surface failed handoffs |
| Customer respect | Keep follow-ups relevant and permission-based; request genuine feedback |
| Practical progress | Start with a recurring problem and measure whether the routine helps |

These values are proposed operating commitments, not verified claims about all current delivery.

## 6. Audience and qualification

Primary buyer hypothesis: a Singapore service-business owner or manager accountable for customer activity, with limited time to coordinate recurring tasks. Workflow need and integration fit are more useful initial criteria than invented ages or incomes.

Existing clients/referrals and wider service SMEs are acquisition pools, not different product definitions. Do not restrict RHAD45 to wellness simply because the older page used that audience.

| Buyer situation | Job to be done | Entry product | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Enquiries arrive while staff serve customers | Acknowledge, capture and hand over details | WhatsApp Lead Response | Volumes, existing process, supported tools and handoff owner |
| Repeat custom matters | Make relevant follow-ups routine | Loyalty Loop | Repeat cycle, permissions and current process |
| Feedback requests are inconsistent | Build genuine review requests into the routine | Review Engine | Current requests, review destinations and approved journey |
| Reports are scattered | See agreed measures and areas needing attention | Reporting & Recommendations | Sources, reporting cadence and decisions to support |

Qualify for a recurring problem, measurable friction, accessible accounts, a responsible approver, compatible workflow and willingness to pay the full confirmed cost. Complex bespoke requests need separate scoping.

Customer interviews should cover the last missed follow-up, current workaround, time/cost implications, approval process and what successful delivery would look like. No customer quotes or interview findings have been supplied yet.

## 7. Positioning proposal

**Stated category:** Business automation products.

**Buyer comparison set:** Manual workarounds, existing software, messaging/CRM platforms and implementation providers. These are alternative categories, not a completed named competitor benchmark.

**Recommended public category:** Practical business automation for Singapore SMEs.

**Positioning statement:** For Singapore service SMEs whose recurring customer tasks compete for staff attention, RHAD45 offers a focused automation product with monitoring and support at S$100/month, with setup and external usage costs agreed separately.

**Internal direction:** Make RHAD45 the understandable entry into RHAD-supported automation. Choose the customer task, agree scope and costs, and establish a supported routine.

**Public explanation:** Choose one everyday business task to put on a better routine. RHAD45 brings practical automation with ongoing monitoring and support, from RHAD.

**Territory:** Dependable everyday follow-through.

Reasons to believe should be a working product demo, transparent terms, a documented handoff and defined support. The S$100 entry price is easy to copy. Defensibility would need to develop through reliable delivery, repeatable workflows, expertise and customer evidence; no established competitive moat is assumed.

For the next research stage, compare customer setup effort against breadth of offer. Place named providers only after inspecting their current official offers. Intended RHAD45 position: focused scope with practical implementation support, subject to delivery confirmation.

**Working hero:** Fewer loose ends. Stronger follow-through.

## 9. Voice direction

Keep RHAD's confidence and focus on commercial value. Use shorter, concrete explanations for SME buyers. Show the task, the change and the next action. Product clarity should carry the promise.

Example: 'Your enquiry flow captures the details. Your team takes the next step.'

Example: 'One selected product. S$100/month. Ongoing monitoring and support.'

The earlier Fred personality is withdrawn. The older superhero/comic language is source context, not a confirmed requirement. Full voice rules and taglines follow strategic review.

## 10. Two-month acquisition target

Ten active subscribers each paying S$100/month yield S$1,000 MRR, excluding separate charges. If customers cancel, more than ten gross additions may be needed to finish with ten active subscribers.

These are planning scenarios, not market benchmarks or forecasts:

| Paid conversion from completed qualified reviews | Reviews needed for 10 subscribers | Average over eight weeks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 20% | 50 | 6.25/week |
| 25% | 40 | 5/week |
| Approximately 33.3% | Approximately 30 | 3.75/week |

A provisional five completed qualified reviews per week makes the 25% scenario concrete. Replace assumptions with actual evidence. Eight weeks is an approximate planning frame; calendar deadlines require the agreed launch date.

Start with existing client/referral conversations and problem-specific service-SME outreach. Confirm activation capacity before expanding acquisition. Track source, qualification, review attendance, selected product, paid conversion, activation, retained MRR and support effort. Outreach has not been sent.

Allocate channel budgets after learning which conversations yield activated, retained subscribers. Paid spend, SEO priorities and specific platform investments require budget and buyer evidence.
